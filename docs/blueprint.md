# **App Name**: Body Factory Gym

## Core Features:

- Hero Section: A compelling opening with a headline, subheadline, and CTA to 'Start Your RM12 Trial,' showcasing the founder's image and gym ambience.
- About Section: Explains Body Factory's HVLP model based on three pillars: Value, Access, Community. Mention clean facilities and 10+ years of operation.
- Founder & Trainer Showcase: Builds trust by showcasing the founder's bio as Mr. Malaysia 2023 Champion and listing three trainers with their expertise.
- Facilities Gallery: Provides visual proof of the gym's three floors and infrared sauna (RM5 extra), with captions for different zones (Cardio, Free Weights, Machines).
- Membership Hub (Preview): Offers transparent pricing with cards for RM12 Walk-In, RM110 Monthly, and RM120 Premium memberships. Includes static 'Join Now (Coming Soon)' buttons with locked feature modals.
- Testimonials: Displays three short reviews highlighting cleanliness, affordability, and community aspects of the gym.
- Contact & Map: Implements a contact form (Name, Phone, Message) and a Google Map of the PJ21 location for easy navigation and inquiries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Metallic Orange (#E75E0D) to convey energy and a fitness-focused aesthetic.
- Background color: Charcoal (#2B2B2B), a near-black, for a modern, high-contrast look. It's desaturated orange, with brightness suitable to the dark color scheme.
- Accent color: Accent Red (#B52100), for call-to-actions, a complementary color providing high contrast against the charcoal background. It's of similar hue to the primary orange but darker and less saturated.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font for a modern, machined, objective, neutral look suitable for headlines and body text.
- A single, long-scroll homepage divided into clear, distinct sections to guide users through the content in a logical flow.
- GSAP / Framer Motion-inspired scroll transitions to smoothly animate content as users scroll through the homepage.
